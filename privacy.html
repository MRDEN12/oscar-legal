Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

1. Summary

Oscar Poster is a private personal tool. It collects no personal data from anyone, and shares nothing with third parties.

2. What is stored and where

  • OAuth access/refresh tokens for the owner's own accounts — stored locally on the owner's own private server, in a file that is never published or committed to public repositories.
  • Video files prepared by the owner — stored locally on the same server until uploaded, then deleted.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no cookies on this website.

3. What is NOT collected

The Tool does not collect names, e-mails, phone numbers, device identifiers or any data about third parties. It has no end users other than its owner.

4. Third parties

Video files are transmitted only to the platform the owner chooses to publish to (TikTok, Instagram), under that platform's own terms and privacy policy. No other third party receives data.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are stored until the owner revokes access in the platform's settings (or deletes them locally). Revoking access in the platform account settings immediately invalidates the stored token.

6. Security

The server is protected by public-key authentication only; tokens are kept outside any public repository. No password-based access is enabled.
